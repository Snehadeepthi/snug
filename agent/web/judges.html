Also try

  • Real body scan (phone): Passport → “Start over” → Scan my body → live camera guide. MediaPipe runs on the phone; photos are never uploaded.
  • No photos? “Tell Snug what you wear” estimates measurements from sizes you own.
  • Store kiosk (concept option 3): open kiosk.html on a laptop, scan (or tap “Use a demo shopper”), then scan the QR with your phone; the passport moves to the phone without touching the server.
  • Several people on one phone: in the Passport tab tap + Add person (e.g. add Demo: Rohan next to Ananya), then scan a tag and see Sizes for everyone; tap a name to switch whose size the agent fetches.
  • 18 stores in 8 metro cities: change the store in the app's Passport tab (or tap “📍 Use my location”); stock, nearby stores, staff screen and manager insights follow the store.
  • Trial-room QR: placards. Scanning a room QR tells Snug which room to send sizes to.
  • Developer view of every agent tool call: ADK dev UI.

What's real: on-device MediaPipe measurement and fit engine, barcode scanning, Gemini agents (Google ADK) with live tool use, staff tasks, Cloud Run. Demo data: fictional brands, catalogue and stores (barcodes use the in-store-only prefix 20–29), demo shopper profiles, and simulated past feedback in the manager view. Privacy: body measurements never leave the phone; the AI sees only fit verdicts.
